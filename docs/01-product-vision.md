# Product Vision

## Vision
Become a trusted trading-intelligence service that continuously interprets market conditions, identifies actionable opportunities, and helps subscribers manage the full lifecycle of a trade through clear evidence, risk boundaries, and timely updates.

## Target user problem
Many traders face noisy markets, conflicting indicators, late entries, unclear invalidation, and inconsistent trade follow-up. A useful product must do more than flag a technical pattern: it must decide whether the complete scenario is actionable and keep evaluating whether it remains valid.

## Value proposition
A subscriber receives a structured opportunity rather than an unexplained alert:
- Instrument and market
- Direction and scenario type
- Market context and supporting evidence
- Entry timing: ENTER NOW, WAIT, MISSED, or REJECT
- Entry zone and maximum acceptable entry distance
- Stop loss / invalidation
- Targets and exit conditions
- Expiry and freshness
- Risk and execution warnings
- Ongoing state changes and final outcome

## Trust principles
1. The system may say NO OPPORTUNITY.
2. Every signal is time-stamped and tied to the data available at that time.
3. Historical signals are never silently edited.
4. Scores are rankings until calibration proves they represent probabilities.
5. Performance reporting includes realistic costs and rejected/expired opportunities.
6. The platform clearly distinguishes observed facts, calculated metrics, and AI interpretation.

## Business model
A multi-tenant SaaS subscription model. The platform generates opportunities centrally; plan entitlements control access by market, trading style, signal tier, delivery channel, and other capabilities once defined. Exact packages and commercial limits are pending approval.

## Success measures (to be finalized)
- Opportunity precision and expectancy after costs
- Drawdown and loss distribution
- Signal freshness and time-to-notification
- Percentage of signals with complete auditable evidence
- Delivery reliability
- Subscriber retention and engagement
- Cost per analyzed opportunity and per active subscriber

No KPI target is approved until the MVP market, trading style, and validation method are defined.
